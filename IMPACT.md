## Application evidence gates

Apply to Codex for OSS only after all readiness checks are true:

- legal, governance, CI, release, security, and evaluation documentation are public;
- at least four weeks of continuous public maintenance are visible;
- at least two releases are at least 21 days apart and their CI is green;
- median maintainer response time for actionable issues/PRs is at most 72 hours;
- either 100 GitHub stars or 50 release downloads in 30 days; alternatively, at least three distinct external users plus one external contribution or actionable quality report.

If the gates are not met after six weeks, continue in two-week maintenance cycles. Never substitute invented usage numbers for evidence.
