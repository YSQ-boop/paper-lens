## Project description

Paper Lens is an Apache-2.0 Codex plugin for trustworthy reading of one academic paper. It prepares arXiv or local PDFs locally, then guides Codex to produce one durable quick/deep report whose claims point to pages, sections, equations, figures, or tables. Deterministic validation rejects placeholders, remote images, weak grounding, and incomplete evidence status.

## Why Codex credits help

Credits would fund reproducible quality evaluations and maintainer PR review, not product telemetry. We test multilingual quick/deep reports, legacy arXiv inputs, local PDFs, source-only operation, and expected failures. A separate judge scores grounding, coverage, uncertainty, formula/table accuracy, and unsupported claims; only reviewed aggregate results are published.

## Public impact and maintenance

Paper Lens is maintained in public with CI, reproducible release artifacts and SHA-256 checksums, security reporting, contributor guidance, and a dated no-telemetry impact record. Before applying we require four weeks of visible maintenance, two releases at least 21 days apart, responsive issue handling, and verified adoption or external-user evidence.
