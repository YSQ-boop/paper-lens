# Roadmap

Paper Lens develops in evidence-gated milestones. Dates are targets, not promises.

## 0.2 — Trustworthy OSS baseline

- Apache-2.0 licensing and contributor/security policy.
- Bounded downloads and archive/media hardening.
- Python 3.10–3.13 and macOS CI coverage.
- Deterministic plus optional model-evaluated quality suite.
- Reproducible tag archives and checksums.

## 0.3 — Quality evidence

- Publish a reviewed baseline over five positive and three negative cases.
- Add public regression cases contributed by users without redistributing paper PDFs.
- Improve formula/table accuracy checks and failure diagnostics.
- Document at least three distinct external user workflows.

## 0.4 — Broader robustness

- Expand coverage for difficult born-digital PDFs and multi-file arXiv sources.
- Stabilize the report contract with versioned migrations.
- Explore opt-in local OCR integration only if it preserves the no-upload boundary.

## Non-goals

- Hidden telemetry or remote analytics.
- A hosted paper-upload service.
- Paywall or access-control bypass.
- Fabricated citations or automatic claims of independent verification.
- Multi-paper literature review before single-paper grounding is dependable.
